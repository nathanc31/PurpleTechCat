About Nathan

👋 Hi, I’m Nathan from L25 and I run PurpleTechCat — friendly, free tech help for children, families and neighbours.

🧒 I’ve spent several years helping in school visits, youth clubs and SEND sports events. I supported deaf and SEND children at Panathlon, where I earned my Young Leader Certificate.

🌈 Before becoming a Tech Volunteer at Switchboard, I was an ambassador for Just Like Us, the national LGBTQ+ young people’s charity. I gave a school talk and received a school talk award.

💛 I volunteered at Deaf Active twice:

  • General Helper (late 2021): youth clubs, playschemes, school visits
  • Volunteer IT Officer (2023–25): staff office digital and software tasks — not child‑facing, but part of a child‑safe environment; won two awards

🎓 I’m studying the BTEC Level 3 National Extended Diploma in IT (2025–27).

🖥️ In 2026, I built my own IT virtualisation lab at home to practise real IT setups.

🔧 I’m now building triple IT experience across:

  • 🏫 a primary school (ICT suite + classroom iPads)
  • 🌈 Switchboard, the national LGBTQ+ helpline charity (remote tech help)
  • 🛠️ an IT support company (new devices + UniFi setups)

🔐 I have a clear Enhanced DBS and always work safely around children and SEND learners.

🤟 I’m deaf, neurodiverse and gay, and I use BSL Levels 1–3.
🐱🍫 And yes — I love cats and chocolate, which is why PurpleTechCat exists.
